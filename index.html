Executive Leadership & Social Impact

SEGUN B. ADEGOKE

visionary social entrepreneur

Segun Adegoke is a visionary social entrepreneur and a changemaker reshaping Nigeria’s real estate and agricultural landscapes.

As the founder and CEO of Baay Projects and Baay Agro, Segun leads with a passion for sustainable development and a commitment to excellence and impact, merging integrity with innovation to create lasting value in the community.

His work has earned him recognition, including a spot in Business Elites Africa’s 40 Under 40, highlighting his dedication to setting new standards for the industries he champions. Not leaving out recognitions as a Ban Ki-moon changemaker, Lord’s Achievers 2024 Awardee, and Tony Elumelu Awardee, amongst others.

International Climate & Policy Delegations

BE HEARD: SEGUN ADEGOKE ON A MISSION OF CHANGE

2024

COP 29 Baku, Azerbaijan

With notable world leaders converging in Baku, Azerbaijan, to discuss global collaboration and the urgency of addressing climate change, over 72,000 delegates from 196 countries gathered to discuss mitigation strategies, adaptation approaches, finance pledges, redemptions, and many others.

2023

COP 28 UAE

At COP28’s first thematic session, H.E. Mariam bin Mohammed Almheiri, UAE Minister of Climate Change and Environment, declared this summit a turning point, emphasizing sustainable agriculture and food systems as vital to tackling climate change and shaping a resilient future.

2023

18th UN Climate Change Conference (COY18)

As a YOUNGO member, this is an opportunity for me to discuss some of our work at @baayagro and @baay projects, network, contribute my quota to some demand papers, and learn more about what others are doing to mitigate climate loss in agriculture and adapt existing cities to the menace of climate change and carbon pollution.
